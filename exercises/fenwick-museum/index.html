Bent Wood, Bent Rules: American Furniture, 1946–1968

March 14 – September 7, 2026 · Fenwick Gallery, second floor

A close look at how a generation of postwar American designers — working with molded plywood, bent steel, and the new economics of mass production — argued that a chair could be both cheap to make and worth keeping. Forty-two objects, most on loan from regional private collections and shown publicly for the first time.

Plan your visit

The Fenwick is a 15-minute walk from Kettleman Station (Blue Line) or a short drive with free parking on Ash Street after 5pm and all day Sunday. The building is fully accessible; wheelchairs are available at the front desk at no charge. Strollers are welcome throughout.
