About this lot

La Cumbre Cooperative sits on the eastern slope of Huila's central range, where a longer, cooler ripening window gives this lot its stone-fruit sweetness. Cherries are hand-picked at peak ripeness and fermented for 36 hours before a fully washed process.

Roast & brew notes

Light-medium roast, developed for clarity over body. Works well as a pour-over or drip; at espresso grind it pulls sweet with low bitterness — pull slightly shorter than usual (1:1.8) if it tastes thin.
