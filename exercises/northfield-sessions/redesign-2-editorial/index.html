No. 12 — Sept 11–13, 2026 Northfield, Minnesota

Northfield Sessions

A three-day festival on the Cannon River fairgrounds, run by the same six people who've run it since 2015, in the town two colleges and a defeated bank robber made famous.

Northfield holds two things at once: a college town's restlessness — Carleton and St. Olaf both sit inside city limits, a few thousand new twenty-year-olds a year — and a milling town's stubbornness, the Cannon River still running past the limestone mills that gave the town its start. Every September since 2015, that mix has produced a festival that books like a college radio station and runs like a grain co-op: efficiently, a little stubbornly, without much interest in what a festival is supposed to look like.

This year's bill leans the same direction it always has — mostly Midwest and upper-Midwest acts, a couple of touring names doing the festival a favor on their way through, two stages close enough together that you can hear both from the beer line.
