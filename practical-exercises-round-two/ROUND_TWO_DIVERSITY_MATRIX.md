## How each risk from the baseline is attacked

**Warm paper ground, 3/3 in round one.** No exercise here uses it. Grounds are: cool near-white (01),
photograph-derived (02), cool neutral (03), saturated mid-tone (04), clinical white (05),
high-contrast light (06), saturated duotone (07), light shell with dark code surfaces (08).

**Editorial/humanist type only.** Round two adds a reading serif built for screen (01), an
accessibility face with a genuine domain reason (05), a semi-condensed engineering grotesque (06), a
variable display face with real personality (07), and an institutional UI face plus a mono that is
justified because the product is code (08).

**Oversized display type as a single move.** Exercises 03, 05, 06 and 08 must succeed on a compressed
scale — roughly 3–4×, with hierarchy from weight, rule, colour and position instead. That is half the
set deliberately denied round one's strongest device.

**One temperature — quiet, print-derived, instrument-like.** 04 and 07 are loud, consumer and
promotional. 05 is soft-spoken but clinical rather than literary. Only 01 sits near round one's
register, and it earns it.

**No photograph anywhere.** 02 makes photography load-bearing and is the round's headline test. 04
adds a second, harder case: seller photographs of *variable quality*, which is what a real resale
marketplace actually looks like and what a designer must handle rather than art-direct away.

**Single-route only.** Every exercise here ships two meaningful routes, since the baseline showed
cross-route coherence was never actually demonstrated by the recent work.

## Asset position, settled before designing

Wikimedia Commons is reachable and returns machine-readable licence and attribution metadata, so 02
and 04 use **real photographs under CC BY-SA or public domain, credited in an asset note per
exercise**. This matters: round one avoided photography by choosing briefs that did not need it, which
solved the symptom and left the capability untested. Two exercises here depend on it.

Where a figure is data rather than a photograph — ice-core stratigraphy, temperature traces, signal
flow — it is authored from real values, as in round one. No blank rectangles, no gradient stand-ins.
