Firn — three structural directions

Schematic only. They differ in reading order, where the apparatus lives, the relationship between figures and text, page depth, and how each collapses on a phone.

S1 · Column with a live core rail

One reading column. Apparatus — depth, year, figure index — lives in a right rail that tracks scroll position, the way a core log is annotated down its length. Figures break the measure at full width.

S2 · Two printed columns

Journal setting: two narrow columns, figures spanning both, apparatus collected in a footer block. Dense and familiar to the audience, but the columns force short measures and the apparatus loses its link to the passage it belongs to.

S3 · Figure spine

Figures are the spine and the prose is commentary hung off them. Reading order is figure first, explanation second. Strong for a photo essay; wrong here, because the argument is continuous prose that the figures support rather than the other way round.
