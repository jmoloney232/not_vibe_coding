A mill that still mills

Bellman's is an eighteenth-century meal mill on the lade below Loch Kindar, half a mile from Sweetheart Abbey and a mile and a half from the Solway shore. There are four rooms.

The wheel is overshot and eleven feet across. When the sluice is up it drives two pairs of stones through the pit wheel and the great spur, and the whole building moves slightly. It is not a museum: we mill oats and we sell the meal at the door.

What that means for you

It is loud on milling days, and it is loud in the specific way that a building full of gearing is loud — a low working noise you feel through the soles of your feet. Some people book because of it. If you are not one of them, come on a Tuesday.

The stairs are mill stairs. The two upper rooms are up open treads with a turn, and there is nowhere in a listed mill to put a lift.
