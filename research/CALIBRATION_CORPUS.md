# Calibration Corpus

Per the methodology note in `RESEARCH_REPORT.md`: this sandbox cannot reach live third-party sites (network egress is allowlisted to npm/pypi/anthropic/github only; direct connection attempts and `WebFetch` both returned 403s on every external production URL tried). So this corpus is honest about what was actually verified versus recalled versus reasoned about, using the label in the **Evidence basis** field for every entry. Entries marked **[OWN, verified]** were actually rendered and screenshotted this session via local Playwright and are the only entries in this corpus backed by first-party visual inspection. Everything else is search-snippet evidence about documented critique/discourse, or (clearly flagged) recalled description from training knowledge.

This corpus is deliberately smaller and shallower than the task's requested 15/15/10 minimums, for the reason stated above: I will not pad it with invented per-example visual detail (exact card counts, exact hex-adjacent color impressions, exact spacing judgments) for sites I have not actually looked at this session — doing so would itself be exactly the "plausible-sounding but unverified specificity" failure mode this whole research task exists to catch. What follows is every entry I can back with real evidence of some kind, organized by classification, with the evidence basis stated for each so a future agent knows exactly how much weight it can bear.

## What this corpus does and doesn't support

**Supports, with real evidence:**
- The "mathematical average of the internet" framing of generic AI-adjacent design (multiple independent sources converge on nearly this exact phrase)
- Purple/blue gradients and shadcn defaults specifically as community-recognized visual fingerprints, with a stated mechanism (the library's own design goal of being AI-copy-pasteable)
- The GOV.UK/Craigslist pair as a clean real-world demonstration that "looks good" and "works well" are separable axes
- That structural template-shape (SaaS page architecture, dashboard-with-cards) is old, converts for real reasons, and is weak evidence of genericness on its own — content interchangeability is the actual test
- That people are demonstrably unreliable at visually detecting AI origin (Monet incident) and that "AI slop" accusations function partly as social gatekeeping rather than verified detection

**Does not support, and should not be cited as if it did:**
- Any specific visual claim about Vercel, Arc, Notion, Framer, or any other named production product not actually re-verified live — this still applies to anything not listed in the round-7 update immediately below
- A claim that any of this project's own outputs "no longer looks AI-generated" — the strongest available evidence (two blind reviewers) found real, unresolved residual signals in the most recent Perigee round, and the honest conclusion is documented ambiguity, not success

## Round 7 update: Stripe and Linear re-verified live — the flag above was correct to make, and the result was a real correction

A later round-7 session had genuine Full network access and re-verified Stripe and Linear directly (real Playwright rendering, real `getComputedStyle` inspection — not search-snippet or recalled evidence; full records in `research/INTERFACE_STUDY_RECORDS.md`, records 3 and 4). The result: both products' primary hero H1 uses negative letter-spacing measuring -0.02em (Stripe) and -0.022em (Linear) — squarely inside the exact range this project's own guide flagged in §8 as reading like "a template tell in its own right." Two more credited, human-designed products (Basecamp, and Varda — a real orbital-manufacturing company) were also measured and land in the same range (-0.0225em and -0.03em respectively). §8 has been corrected in `ANTI_VIBE_CODING_UI.md` as a direct result. This is exactly the outcome this corpus's closing note anticipated needing to check, and the fact that verification produced a real correction (not just a confirmation) is itself evidence the `[RECALLED]`/`[SEARCH]`-grade caution above was the right call, not excessive hedging.

**Live-verified evidence tier added:** entries in `research/INTERFACE_STUDY_RECORDS.md` use **[LIVE]** to mean genuinely rendered, screenshotted, and DOM/CSS-inspected this session — a stronger tier than `[OWN]` as originally defined (which referred only to this project's own built artifacts) and strictly stronger than `[SEARCH]`/`[RECALLED]`. Ten records (five Corpus A, three Corpus B, two Corpus C) now carry this tier; see that file for full detail, including a real counter-instance to the round-6 corner-bracket/technical-framing open question (Varda's dotted grid-overlay hero) and direct evidence that AI-tool-generated output (three Lovable/v0 templates, all disclosed-provenance) is not visually monolithic.
